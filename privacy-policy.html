Contact form. When you submit the "Send a Message" form on this Site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, subject, and message content. This is sent to us via EmailJS, a third-party form-processing service, and delivered to our email inbox.

We use the information you submit through the contact form for one purpose only: to read your message and reply to you. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not add you to any mailing list unless you explicitly ask us to.

We retain contact form submissions (as email messages) for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, and no longer than 24 months unless a longer period is needed for legal or record-keeping reasons.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at kankanstudios.support@gmail.com.
